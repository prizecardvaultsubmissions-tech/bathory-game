SURVIVAL CODEX

  • Attack: Hold the big ATTACK button. You auto-turn toward the nearest servant in reach. Ghouls, Blood Maidens and Bat Swarms rise from the mist quickly, more as the night drags on.
  • Drops: A slain servant may leave a sanity vial, a mirror, a coin pouch, or nothing. Walk over a drop before it fades (~20s).
  • The Well: Toss 5 coins into the Outer Ward well. The offering may grant a 15s speed boost, a 15s darkness curse (your lantern shrinks), or wake a Well Dweller — a crawling wraith that also rises from the well on its own. Her presence drains Sanity.
  • Secret doors: A few rooms hide a passage behind a curious item (a chapel candelabra, a skull, a crooked portrait). Search it and the wall will grind open. Found passages appear on the minimap.
  • Crates & barrels: Smash them (a few hits; the mace splits them in one or two). Inside: a weapon change token (pick a new weapon), an armor plate (the ARMOR bar soaks monster hits), a 15s speed tonic, or a crate slime that splits into two minis when it dies.
  • Kills & keys: The ☠ counter tracks kills. At 15 kills Key I falls; at 30, Key II. Keys glow in the HUD.
  • Locked chambers: Two sealed doors off the south of the Outer Ward. Key I opens the Chamber of Candles; Key II opens the Crushing Hall. The door locks behind you and a riddle appears (tap RIDDLE to read it again). Candles: light all six in the riddle's order; a wrong flame snuffs them all. Crushing Hall: press four glyph plates in order before the walls meet; a wrong plate makes them lurch closer. Neither the Countess nor new servants can reach you inside. Solve it for a rich reward.
  • The Hooded Pedlar: A hooded merchant keeps a stall in a quiet corner of every stage and also meets you between stages. Tap SHOP. Supplies: health draught, sanity salts, lamp oil, armor, mirrors, bolts, speed tonic, weapon swap. Permanent blessings (Iron Heart, Whetstone, Wider Wick, Silvered Glass) are kept forever. The game pauses while you trade.
  • Coins & your purse: Coins carry forward from stage to stage and are saved in your purse. If you die you keep the coins you carried in (minus anything you spent) plus half of what you picked up that run.
  • Stage II · The Graveyards: Escape the castle with all five pages to unlock it (then use CONTINUE on the title screen). Take five grave rubbings across six yards and leave by the Gatehouse road. Grave-risen skeletons claw out of the earth, Gravedigger Brutes hit hard, and Banshees wail to drain sanity and slow you. Open graves have hands — keep your distance. Coffins and urns break like crates. The black fountain takes offerings like the well. Keys fall at 15 and 30 kills again for the Crypt of Names and the Drowned Mausoleum.
  • Stage III · The Climb: Escape the Graveyards to unlock it. Six floors of the tower, one above the other: the Cellar & Wine Vaults, the Torture Chambers, the Servants' Floor, the Library & Study, the Countess's Bedchamber and the Tower Top. Every floor's stair up is barred until you break that floor's Tower Seal; tap CLIMB / DESCEND at a stair. She follows you a few seconds later, and she grows faster and sharper-eyed the higher you go. New foes: Chained Prisoners, armored Iron Maiden Walkers (strike while their doors are open), Portrait Wraiths that blink toward you, and bat swarms. Casks, crates and bookshelves break. The Pedlar keeps a stall on the Servants' Floor. On the roof, kill the Tower Gargoyle (it swoops — sidestep) to open the dawn stair. Keys at 15 and 30 kills open the Hall of Mirrors (turn four glasses, then open the lamp) and the timed Clock Library (pull the hour-books in order).
  • Graphics: Tap GRAPHICS on the title or pause screen to cycle AUTO / LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH. AUTO drops quality if your frame rate falls.
  • The Countess: She cannot be harmed. Blows only make her recoil. If she catches you in the open, you die.
  • Mirrors: Only a few lie in the castle — most come from fallen servants. Tap MIRROR when she's close to freeze her, or one shatters automatically if she catches you. Cracked 3s · Silver 6s · Gilded 10s · Obsidian 15s (also repels monsters).
  • Lantern & Stealth: Light lets you see but she sees it too. DOUSE to vanish, though darkness drains Sanity.
  • Iron Alcoves: Tap HIDE to vanish from her and the monsters alike.
  • Braziers: Refill Lamp Oil, Sanity and slowly mend Health.
  • Flare: 360° burst (20% oil) that stuns her if close. Cracked glass rings out and draws her near.
  • Goal: Five collectibles lie across each stage (plus a gate guardian in the Woods, Courtyard, and Tower Roof). Gather all five, defeat any guardian, and escape north!
