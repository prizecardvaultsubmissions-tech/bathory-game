CSEJTE:
BLOOD LEDGER

Six wings of a cursed castle. Arm yourself, cut down her risen servants, gather mirrors, and find five ledger pages before the Blood Countess finds you.

☾ WITCHING HOUR ACTIVE — THE COUNTESS WAKES FASTER

Curse Alerts are 100% harmless & opt-in local whispers. Keys: WASD move · Space attack · E act · F douse · Q flare · R mirror.

CHOOSE YOUR ARMS

THE ARMORY OF CSEJTE

Weapons cut down her servants. Nothing you carry can wound the Countess; only mirrors hold her.

SURVIVAL CODEX

  • Attack: Hold the big ATTACK button. You auto-turn toward the nearest servant in reach. Ghouls, Blood Maidens and Bat Swarms rise from the mist quickly, more as the night drags on.
  • Drops: A slain servant may leave a sanity vial, a mirror, a coin pouch, or nothing. Walk over a drop before it fades (~20s).
  • The Well: Toss 5 coins into the Outer Ward well. The offering may grant a 15s speed boost, a 15s darkness curse (your lantern shrinks), or wake a Well Dweller — a crawling wraith that also rises from the well on its own. Her presence drains Sanity.
  • Secret doors: A few rooms hide a passage behind a curious item (a chapel candelabra, a skull, a crooked portrait). Search it and the wall will grind open. Found passages appear on the minimap.
  • The Countess: She cannot be harmed. Blows only make her recoil. If she catches you in the open, you die.
  • Mirrors: Only a few lie in the castle — most come from fallen servants. Tap MIRROR when she's close to freeze her, or one shatters automatically if she catches you. Cracked 3s · Silver 6s · Gilded 10s · Obsidian 15s (also repels monsters).
  • Lantern & Stealth: Light lets you see but she sees it too. DOUSE to vanish, though darkness drains Sanity.
  • Iron Alcoves: Tap HIDE to vanish from her and the monsters alike.
  • Braziers: Refill Lamp Oil, Sanity and slowly mend Health.
  • Flare: 360° burst (20% oil) that stuns her if close. Cracked glass rings out and draws her near.
  • Goal: Five ledger pages lie across six wings. Find them all, then escape through the North Gate. The map (top right) marks pages in gold.
